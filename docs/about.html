About Toronto Ward Facts

Toronto Ward Facts is a non-partisan, static website that presents official City of Toronto data about the 2022 municipal election for each of Toronto's 25 wards. Every number on this site comes directly from a City of Toronto open-data file. Nothing has been estimated or modelled.

What this site shows

  • 2022 voter turnout by ward (registered voters, ballots cast, turnout %).
  • 2022 official councillor results: top 5 candidates with vote counts and percentages.
  • Current councillor, with a note where a by-election changed the seat after 2022.
  • Links to the City's 2026 candidate list and voter tools – no candidate names or promises are copied here.

How Numbers Are Calculated

Registered voters

The "Total Eligible Electors" column from the voter statistics file, summed across all voting subdivisions (1–96) within each ward. Subdivisions 97 (mail-in), 98 and 99 (advance vote locations) do not carry their own eligible-elector count – those voters are already counted in their home subdivisions – so they are excluded from the eligible total to avoid double-counting.

Ballots cast

The sum of "Number Voted" and "Declined Ballots" from all subdivisions (including 97, 98, 99), per ward. This matches the City's own definition of turnout in the file's readme tab.

Turnout %

Ballots cast ÷ Registered voters × 100, rounded to two decimal places. Formula as described in the file's readme: "Percentage of voters that voted. Number Voted plus Declined Ballots divide by Total Eligible Electors."

Candidate percentages

Each candidate's votes ÷ total votes cast in that ward (councillor race only) × 100. The "Total" column from the City's poll-by-poll spreadsheet is used directly.

Limitations

  • All turnout data is from the 2022 general election. Ward populations and eligible voter counts change year to year.
  • This site does not show 2026 candidates or results (those become available from the City's open data after election night).
  • Candidate name formatting follows the City's own files (last name first in some fields); this site reproduces the names as they appear in the official data.
