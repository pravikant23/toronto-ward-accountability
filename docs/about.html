About Toronto Ward Facts

What this site is

Toronto Ward Facts presents official municipal election and census data for all 25 Toronto wards. It is non-partisan and has no affiliation with any candidate, party, or the City of Toronto. Every number comes from a City of Toronto or Statistics Canada open data file. Nothing is estimated or invented.

What matters here — methodology

The "What matters here" section on each ward page shows ward-level data for the four issues Toronto residents named most often in a September 2026 survey.

  • Traffic and roads: 311 service request counts for potholes, road damage, traffic signals, sidewalks, and related categories (2022–2026). Request type names were renamed by the City in 2025; pre-2025 equivalent names are mapped for consistent year-over-year counting. KSI collision counts use unique collision IDs where the outcome was a fatality or serious injury.
  • Housing costs: Renter share and median household income from the 2021 Census. Building permits per ward are not available: the City's permits file uses a map-grid reference code that does not correspond to ward boundaries.
  • Public safety: Five MCI categories (Assault, Auto Theft, Break and Enter, Robbery, Theft Over) by date of occurrence. Ward assignment uses a spatial join of WGS84 coordinates against ward polygons (1–2% of records dropped per year due to missing coordinates). 2025 data covers January–September only.
  • Property taxes: Rates are set city-wide by City Council. Per-ward tax-rate data does not exist. The section links to the councillor's budget vote record.

Poll source: Nanos Research for CTV News/CP24, Sept 30–Oct 4, 2026. n=755, MOE ±3.6%, 19 times out of 20.

Turnout formula

Turnout % = (Number Voted + Declined Ballots) ÷ Total Eligible Electors × 100

This matches the City's own definition in the voter statistics file. Subdivisions 97 (mail-in), 98, and 99 (advance voting) are included in the vote count but not in the eligible-elector total, because those voters are already counted in their home subdivision's eligible total.

City-wide 2022: 1,898,750 registered, 563,563 ballots cast, 29.7% turnout.
