About Toronto Ward Facts

What this site is

Toronto Ward Facts presents official municipal election and census data for all 25 Toronto wards. It is non-partisan and has no affiliation with any candidate, party, or the City of Toronto. Every number comes from a City of Toronto or Statistics Canada open data file. Nothing is estimated or invented.

What matters here — methodology

The "What matters here" section on each ward page shows ward-level data for the four issues Toronto residents named most often in a September 2026 survey.

  • Traffic and roads: 311 service request counts for potholes, road damage, traffic signals, sidewalks, and related categories (2025 full year shown; 2026 Jan–Sep shown separately). The City renamed its 311 service categories in 2025, so earlier years cannot be compared directly and are not shown. KSI collision counts use unique collision IDs where the outcome was a fatality or serious injury. 2025 KSI is full year (Jan–Dec); 2026 KSI covers Jan–Sep only.
  • Housing costs: Renter share and median household income from the 2021 Census. Language spoken most often at home (excluding English and French) from the 2021 Census 25% sample. Building permits per ward are not available: the City's permits file uses a map-grid reference code that does not correspond to ward boundaries.
  • Public safety: Five MCI categories (Assault, Auto Theft, Break and Enter, Robbery, Theft Over) by date of occurrence. Ward assignment uses a spatial join of WGS84 coordinates against ward polygons (1–2% of records dropped per year due to missing coordinates). 2025 data covers January–September only (the full dataset does not include Oct–Dec 2025). A Jan–Sep 2024 comparison is provided for each ward and city-wide. Downtown wards have many commuters and visitors, so offences per resident can look higher.
  • Property taxes: Rates are set city-wide by City Council under Ontario's strong-mayor rules (since 2023). Per-ward tax-rate data does not exist. The section shows the councillor's vote on tax rates and fees for each budget year (2023–2026), including which sub-vote is shown.

Poll source: Nanos Research for CTV News/CP24, Sept 30–Oct 4, 2026. n=755, MOE ±3.6%, 19 times out of 20.

Turnout formula

Turnout % = Number Voted ÷ Total Eligible Electors × 100

Ballots cast = Number Voted only (column 14 of the voter statistics file). Declined ballots are not included. Subdivisions 97 (mail-in), 98, and 99 (advance voting) are included in the vote count but not in the eligible-elector total, because those voters are already counted in their home subdivision's eligible total.

City-wide 2022: 1,898,750 registered, 563,124 ballots cast, 29.7% turnout.

Editorial independence

Toronto Ward Facts is a civic project by Perimtr, an independent research and software company based in Toronto.

This site is non-partisan and is not affiliated with any candidate, political party, or the City of Toronto. All data on this site comes from City of Toronto open data sources. Editorial decisions — what to show, how to describe it, and what to omit — are made independently by the Perimtr team.

We do not accept advertising, sponsorship, or payment from any political organization. If you believe a number is wrong, please send corrections to ravi@perimtr.ca.
