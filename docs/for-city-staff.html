Toronto Ward Facts — Staff Briefing

Thank you for reviewing this site before public release. This page explains what the site does, what data it uses, and how to flag corrections or concerns.

What this site is

Toronto Ward Facts is a non-partisan civic information site that presents official City of Toronto open data in plain language for residents ahead of the October 26, 2026 municipal election. It covers all 25 wards.

Each ward page shows: 2022 voter turnout and election results, councillor attendance and voting record on Council (2022–2026), census profile, and ward-level data on traffic, crime, and 311 service requests.

Each councillor page shows: every close council vote the member participated in, with a plain-language summary of the agenda item and the vote result.

Data sources

Every number on this site comes from a City of Toronto open data file, published under the Open Government Licence – Toronto. No numbers have been estimated, adjusted, or inferred.

What to review

We would welcome your review of the following:

  1. Numbers: Do the voter turnout figures, candidate results, and council vote counts match your records?
  2. Fairness: Is every councillor described in the same way, with no editorial judgment about whether their record is good or bad?
  3. Plain language: Is the methodology explanation on the About page accurate and clear?
  4. Council voting summaries: The one-sentence summaries of agenda items on councillor pages are generated from the official item title in the voting CSV. If any summary mischaracterizes an item, please flag it.
  5. Links: All external links point to toronto.ca or Statistics Canada. Let us know if any are broken or misdirected.

How to send feedback

Email corrections and concerns to ravi@perimtr.ca. Please include:

  • The page URL and the specific figure or text in question
  • What the correct value or wording should be, and the source

We will acknowledge corrections within 48 hours and update the site before public launch.

Our goal is to help Toronto residents make informed choices on election day using only official City data.
