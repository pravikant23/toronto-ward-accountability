What This Platform Does

Toronto voters often ask: "Did my neighbourhood actually improve? What did my councillor do? Where did the money go?"

This platform gives you a single, data-driven view of each of Toronto's 25 wards:

📊 Election Data

2022 election results, top candidates, and vote counts—see how competitive the race was.

🏘️ Neighbourhood Changes

Schools, transit, green space, and safety metrics year-over-year.

💰 Budget Snapshot

How the councillor spent discretionary funds and what major projects they brought in.

🏗️ Development Pipeline

What got rezoned, approved, or built during their term.

💬 Community Voice

Comment on your ward's progress and engage with neighbours.

✅ Fully Transparent

Every fact is sourced from Toronto Open Data—no opinions, just verified data.
